# Impact analysis: deterministic Grant research judgment boundary

## Problem

Structured sources alone do not prevent the model from repeating content that
the application already contains or from promoting an adjacent literature
trend into a new research work package. Existing-design coverage and evidence
strength were also represented as one concept, allowing a planned method or a
performance figure to be described as completed mechanistic proof.

## Authority change

- The existing research-gap comparison remains the only owner that maps recent
  evidence to frozen application locations.
- Located designs now separate coverage level, verification status and explicit
  support boundaries.
- A new deterministic Grant Research Scope Decision Policy exclusively owns
  relevance, scope impact and final suggestion disposition.
- Models propose bounded signals and explanations only. They cannot promote a
  rejected candidate or overwrite the calculated decision.
- Rejected candidates remain auditable and do not enter final synthesis.

## Compatibility and rollout

This changes only target research-judgment contracts and offline verification.
It does not write a canonical Revision, alter diagnostics, call a paid Provider,
change database rows or enable a production route. Formal orchestration and
user-facing synthesis adopt these contracts in the output-layer integration
step, with no parallel comparison implementation.

## Verification

- A located planned design preserves its verification and support boundary.
- A verified no-gap candidate creates no recommendation and is audited as
  `already_covered`.
- An adjacent lifecycle work package is rejected even when model prose proposes
  adding it.
- A direct, bounded extension of an existing method remains eligible as a main
  suggestion.
